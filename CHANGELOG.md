# Changelog

## 1.1.0
Upkeep for the frozen heap, one event per operation, and two cost fixes. All options are in Configure and apply at once; every existing sensor, service and response is unchanged.

Why: the 24 hours measured after 1.0.0 (one instance, 7,200 entities, Python 3.14.10) cut pauses of 1 s or more from 7.7 to 0.42 an hour, but two things were left. (1) The shortest full collection grew from 0.28 s one hour after the freeze to 0.56 s after 19 hours, because objects created after the freeze stay in the unfrozen generation the collector walks every time. (2) Each pause above 1 s sat after a gap of 1.3 to 3.4 hours without a full collection, and its length is the garbage that built up in that gap. Both are about time, not about the thresholds.

- Daily maintenance (default on, 04:30): unfreeze, collect, freeze. Reclaims objects that were frozen and have died since and starts the day with a small unfrozen heap. One pause of about 2 to 3 seconds a day. New service `gc_tuning.maintain` runs the same on command.
- Periodic re-freeze (default every 6 hours): collect and freeze, on a wall-clock grid anchored at the maintenance time (the interval is rounded to a divisor of 24). It runs only once a full collection that Python started itself has taken at least the pause target (default 500 ms) since the last freeze, so nothing is paid while the unfrozen heap is still small; 0 means every slot runs. With the daily maintenance off, the slots do the full maintenance instead, so frozen-then-dead objects cannot pile up.
- A full collection at least every N minutes (default 45; 0 = off, otherwise 5 to 1440): small and frequent instead of rare and large. The check runs once a minute; any full collection, Python's or ours, restarts the clock.
- Event `gc_tuning_operation` after every operation, once the sensors show it: `action`, `collected`, `frozen_before`, `frozen_after`, `duration_ms`, `at`. Actions: `startup_apply`, `apply_service`, `freeze_service`, `unfreeze_service`, `collect_service`, `maintain_service`, `daily_maintenance`, `periodic_maintenance`, `periodic_refreeze`, `periodic_collection`, `retune`, `restore`. `frozen_before` is the last count read, at most 5 minutes old.
- Four sensors: Last operation (state = action, attributes = the event data), Last operation duration, Last maintenance at, Longest full collection since freeze (the value the pause target is compared with; the collections the integration runs itself are not counted).
- The upkeep acts only while the tuning is enabled, applied and the heap was frozen by this integration; disabling or unloading cancels every timer.
- Cost fix: `gc.get_freeze_count()` walks the whole permanent generation (20 ms for 2.6 million objects on a quiet machine, several times that on a busy one) and holds the GIL. 1.0.0 called it every minute for the Frozen objects sensor and again inside the log line of every slow collection. 1.1.0 reuses one read for 5 minutes, reads again after an operation that changes the frozen set, and the slow-collection warning prints the last count read. The `stats` service still reads fresh.
- Defaults: the thresholds stay 50000 / 10 / 10. 10000 / 10 / 10 was tried live on the instance for 6 minutes (2026-11-20 17:23:17 to 17:29:09, then set back): a burst of allocations that began at about 17:27 produced 13 full collections of 0.73 to 0.91 s in 110 s (10.9 s of pause) with 10000, each paying the walk of the unfrozen heap; the burst's cause was not identified and it is one burst, not a controlled comparison. Once the heap is frozen a full collection starts after generation 0 threshold x generation 1 x generation 2 net allocations, so lowering the first number five-fold makes full collections in a burst five times as frequent. Without evidence for a better value, the value measured for 24 hours stays; the threshold is a live option for a longer trial. Reset to the proven defaults now means the defaults above.
- An entry stored by 1.0.0 needs no migration: the new options read as their defaults until the form is saved once.

## 1.0.0
- First release as an installable integration (config flow, options, sensors, diagnostics, icon). The 0.1.0 module lived in a Home Assistant config repository and was YAML only.
- Behaviour by default is the proven fix of 0.1.0: 300 s after Home Assistant has started, one full collection, `gc.freeze()` of the start-up objects, thresholds 50000 / 10 / 10.
- A `gc_tuning:` YAML block of 0.1.0 is imported once into a config entry (`auto_apply` becomes `enabled`, `delay` becomes `start_delay`); the YAML block can then be removed.
- Options (changes apply at once, no restart): enabled, start delay (60-1800 s), generation 0 threshold (700-200000), generations 1 and 2 (1-100), freeze, log limit, and a reset to the proven defaults.
- Seven diagnostic sensors: full collections in the last hour, longest pause in the last hour, last full collection duration, frozen objects, thresholds, applied at, process memory (RSS).
- Diagnostics download; English translations; icon shipped in `brand/` (Home Assistant 2026.3 and later).
- The five services (`apply`, `freeze`, `unfreeze`, `collect`, `stats`) and the `stats` response are unchanged.
